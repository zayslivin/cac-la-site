# Stop Slop

Strip AI writing tells before finalizing any user-facing copy for CCLA. Rewrite
into plain, concrete language that matches the site's existing voice: upscale
but direct, confident, no filler (see the tone of existing event pages —
short declarative lines, specifics like prices/dates/venue names, not vague
hype).

Cut or rewrite on sight:
- Em-dashes used to join clauses for dramatic effect
- Stock openers/closers: "In today's fast-paced world," "Let's dive in,"
  "In conclusion," "At the end of the day"
- Hype verbs/nouns: "elevate," "unleash," "game-changer," "unlock,"
  "revolutionize," "seamless," "curated experience" (unless literally true
  and specific)
- Rule-of-three padding used as a crutch ("vibrant, dynamic, unforgettable")
- Hedges that add no information: "it's worth noting," "it's important to
  remember," "needless to say"
- Generic AI-ish transitions: "That said," "With that in mind," "Moving
  forward"

Prefer: short sentences, specific nouns (venue name, price, date, spot count)
over adjectives, and active voice. If a sentence would work equally well in
any other brand's copy, make it specific to CCLA or cut it.
