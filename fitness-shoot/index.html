Before You Book

Questions, answered.

What's included?

A one-on-one fitness shoot in a San Bernardino gym, and 20 professionally edited images. $250 total.

Why is there a deposit?

The $125 deposit holds your shoot and counts toward the $250. You pay the other $125 on shoot day.

How do I pick a date?

Add your preferred days and times at checkout. Then DM @createconnect.la on Instagram and we'll lock in a date that works.

What should I wear?

Bring 2–3 fitted training looks you feel strong in. Solid colors photograph best, and black always works. Come warmed up and ready to move.

Who's behind the camera?

Aurea Nat Photo (@aureanatphoto), who shot every image on this page.
