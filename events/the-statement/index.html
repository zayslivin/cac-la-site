Before You RSVP

Questions, answered.

What's included?

Three hours in a private multi-set Los Angeles studio with every set available to you and every photographer, model, videographer and artist in the room. Confirmed attendees get event details by email before the day. Photographers/creators $95, models $55.

What should I bring?

Photographers: your camera and a fast lens or two. The sets are lit, but bring your own lighting if you prefer it. Models: 2–4 statement looks. Videographers: your rig, because the sets work for motion too. Come with a concept, or build one on the day.

What's the dress code?

Make a statement. Editorial and high-impact: sculptural shapes, patent and vinyl, chrome, monochrome red or black, bold color-blocking, and the one accessory people remember. If it could hold a magazine cover on its own, it fits.

Where's the exact location?

It's a private multi-set studio in Los Angeles. The exact studio, address and parking instructions go to confirmed attendees by email before event day.

How do I RSVP and pay?

RSVP through our secure checkout, which reserves your spot and takes payment in one step. Photographers/creators $95, models $55. Spots are first come, first served, and the room is capped at 20.

How do I get final images?

Photographers keep their files. Most share previews within 48 hours and edited galleries within 2 weeks. Models can DM photographers directly to license or use specific frames.
