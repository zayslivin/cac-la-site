Before You RSVP

Questions, answered.

What's included?

3 hours in a private multi-set Los Angeles studio with access to every set, access to all photographers, models, videographers, and artists in the room, and the pre-event group chat with the full attendee list, concepts, and styling notes. Photographers/creators $95, models $55.

What should I bring?

Photographers: your camera and a fast lens or two — sets are lit, but bring your own lighting if you prefer it. Models: 2–4 looks that play against different sets. Videographers: your rig — the sets are built for motion too. Come with a concept, or build one on the day.

What's the dress code?

Barbie World, Ken Core, and everything around it — pink, pastel, retro, chrome, denim, Y2K, colour-blocked. Anything that pops against a candy-bright set. A moodboard and full styling direction are shared in the group chat before event day.

Where's the exact location?

It's a private multi-set studio in Los Angeles. The exact studio, address, and parking instructions are released to confirmed attendees after RSVP.

How do I RSVP and pay?

RSVP through our secure checkout — it handles your spot and payment in one step. Photographers/creators $95, models $55. Spots are first come, and the room is capped at 35.

How do I get final images?

Photographers retain their files. Most share previews within 48 hours and edited galleries within 2 weeks via the event group chat. Models can DM photographers directly to license/use specific frames.
