The Concept

25 creatives.
One rain room.

Creative Day is an afternoon in a multi-set Los Angeles studio for models, photographers and videographers. A rain set lit in blue and magenta, a laser wall, strip-light walls and a bulb wall — every corner built to be shot.

The room is capped at twenty-five, so you get real time on every set and with every person in it, instead of waiting your turn.

Three hours and a room of people who take the work seriously. Come with a concept, or build one with whoever you meet on set.

Before You RSVP

Questions, answered.

What's included?

Three hours in a private multi-set Los Angeles studio, including the rain set, with every set open to you and every model, photographer and videographer in the room. Confirmed attendees get event details by email before the day. Photographers and videographers $95, models $55.

What should I bring?

Photographers: your camera and a fast lens or two, plus a rain cover if you plan to shoot close to the rain set. Videographers: your rig — the rain and light walls are made for motion. Models: 2–4 looks, and a towel and a look you don't mind getting wet if you want to step into the rain.

Is there a theme or dress code?

No strict theme. The sets are lit in blue, magenta, laser pink and warm bulb light, so looks with strong color, shine or texture read well on camera. Come styled to be shot.

Where's the exact location?

It's a private multi-set studio in Los Angeles. The exact studio, address and parking instructions go to confirmed attendees by email before event day.

How do I RSVP and pay?

RSVP through our secure checkout, which reserves your spot and takes payment in one step. Photographers and videographers $95, models $55. Spots are first come, first served, and the room is capped at 25.

How do I get final images?

Photographers and videographers keep their files. Most share previews within 48 hours and edited galleries within 2 weeks. Models can DM photographers directly to license or use specific frames.
