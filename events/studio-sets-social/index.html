The Concept

16 sets.
One afternoon.

This room moves into a multi-set Los Angeles studio — 16 fully built, themed sets under one roof. A retro diner, a neon graffiti tunnel, a white sci-fi corridor, a color-blocked classroom, a tropical lounge, and more — every wall pre-lit, pre-styled, and ready to shoot.

This is not a meetup. It's a curated room of 30 photographers, models, and videographers — chosen for taste, intention, and the energy that produces magazine-grade work in three hours.

Rotate through the sets, pair up organically, and stack an entire portfolio's worth of distinct looks in a single afternoon. Sixteen worlds; you'll want a plan.

Before You RSVP

Questions, answered.

What's included?

3 hours in a private multi-set studio with access to all 16 themed sets, access to all photographers, models, and videographers in the room, and the pre-event group chat with the full attendee list, concepts, and styling notes. Photographers/creators $95, models $55.

What should I bring?

Photographers: your camera, a fast lens or two — sets are pre-lit, but bring your own lighting if you prefer it. Models: 2–4 looks that play against different sets (we'll share a set list and styling direction in advance). Videographers: your rig — the sets are built for motion too.

What's the dress code?

Styled for the sets. Think bold color, retro, streetwear, and futuristic looks — anything that pops against a diner booth, a graffiti tunnel, or a white sci-fi corridor. A moodboard with the full set list is shared in the group chat before event day.

Where's the exact location?

It's a private multi-set studio in Los Angeles. The exact studio, address, and parking instructions are released to confirmed attendees after RSVP.

How do I RSVP and pay?

RSVP through our secure checkout — it handles your spot and payment in one step. Photographers/creators $95, models $55. Spots are first come, and the room is capped at 30.

How do I get final images?

Photographers retain their files. Most share previews within 48 hours and edited galleries within 2 weeks via the event group chat. Models can DM photographers directly to license/use specific frames.
