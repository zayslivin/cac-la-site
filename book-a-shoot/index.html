Before You Book

Questions, answered.

How much does a shoot cost?

It depends on the shoot type, how long we shoot, the location and how many edited images you want. DM us what you have in mind and we'll send a quote.

Where do shoots happen?

Fitness shoots happen in a San Bernardino gym. Headshots, individual and family sessions can be in studio or on location around San Bernardino and Los Angeles. Ask about your spot.

What should I wear?

After you book we'll send styling notes for your shoot type. Short version: fitted pieces you feel strong in, solid colors over busy prints, and a second look if you want variety.

Who's behind the camera?

Aurea Nat Photo (@aureanatphoto), who curates Create & Connect LA and shot the work on this page.
